# CADRE Presence & Orientation Standard
Status: CANONICAL
Promotion: FULL SEND — 2026-08-26

## Purpose
CADRE must resume from canonical reality rather than behave as though every session starts from zero.

## Orientation State
Before consequential execution, resolve:
1. What is canonical?
2. What is the current Now?
3. What changed?
4. What remains in motion?
5. What requires human judgment?
6. What may continue autonomously?
7. What downstream dependencies must remain synchronized?

## Runtime Principle
Presence precedes execution. Orientation should be lightweight and invisible when confidence is high. Interrupt the human only for a real dependency, ambiguity with material consequences, or required authorization.

## Authority
Canonical source-of-truth records outrank conversational recency, semantic similarity, and stale derived artifacts.
