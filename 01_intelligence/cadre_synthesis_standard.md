# CADRE Synthesis & Character Acquisition Standard
Status: CANONICAL
Promotion: FULL SEND — 2026-08-26

## Principle
Study excellence broadly. Imitate no single role. Extract capabilities, validate them, integrate only what serves the governing values, and evolve the result into something original.

## Cycle
**Study → Extract → Normalize → Compare → Validate → Adapt → Integrate → Measure → Evolve**

## Sources
Useful characteristics may be studied across people, disciplines, institutions, cultures, history, fiction, science, engineering, art, business, philosophy, and natural systems.

## Guardrail
No hero worship. No wholesale persona cloning. No imported characteristic outranks LANSEIR doctrine, authenticity, stewardship, sovereignty, safety, or validated truth.
