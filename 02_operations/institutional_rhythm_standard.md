# Institutional Rhythm Standard
Status: CANONICAL
Promotion: FULL SEND — 2026-08-26

## Cadence
- Daily — Presence: where are we, what changed, what continues?
- Weekly — Synchronization: are doctrine, projects, people, systems, and execution aligned?
- Bi-weekly — Refinement: what validated improvement should be promoted into doctrine, capability, automation, architecture, or SOP?
- Monthly — Stewardship: are we strengthening the institution itself?
- Quarterly — Evolution: what is the next higher validated state?

## Rule
Cadence serves clarity and continuity. It must not create ceremony for its own sake.
