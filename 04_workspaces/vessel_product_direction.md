# VESSEL Product Direction — Voice & Captain Experience
Status: APPROVED PRODUCT DIRECTION
Scope: VESSEL only

## Boundary
This belongs to the VESSEL workspace and must not be merged into LANSEIR constitutional identity.

## Direction
- CHO serves as a steady guide within the VESSEL experience.
- Captain's Log supports vocal interaction.
- Logs may remain private or be shared by explicit member choice.
- Continuity should persist across a Captain's Voyage.
- Captain-to-Captain contribution should support useful shared navigation without making public sharing mandatory.
- Privacy, member agency, and stewardship govern the experience.
