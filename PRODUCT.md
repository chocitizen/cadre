# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CADRE begins as a private owner-operated platform for Cho Zen Dell. It must also remain understandable and operable by explicitly authorized administrators, maintainers, and successors without depending on undocumented founder knowledge. Users enter from desktop or mobile to direct work, recover prior context, review approvals, and open exact deliverables.

## Product Purpose

CADRE is the sovereign parent intelligence, governance, workspace, artifact, automation, and application-services platform for the ecosystem. It gives each governed workspace a durable place for conversations, decisions, jobs, artifacts, notifications, provenance, and audit history while preserving human authority and canonical source boundaries.

Foundation v0.1 succeeds when an authenticated owner can select a workspace, hold and reopen a persistent AI conversation, create and reopen a durable Markdown artifact, and retrieve completed work through the Ready Dock. VESSEL is an initial workspace and future flagship application; it is not CADRE itself.

## Brand Personality

Sovereign, majestic, and lucid. CADRE should feel composed under pressure: quiet confidence, disciplined command, visible authority, and technical precision without coldness or spectacle.

## Anti-references

CADRE must not resemble a generic AI chat clone, a noisy enterprise dashboard, a gaming interface, or a decorative terminal. Avoid loud gradients, glassmorphism, excessive cards, gimmicky nautical motifs, ornamental luxury cues, unexplained automation, and interfaces that hide authority or provenance behind visual polish.

## Design Principles

1. Make authority legible: source classification, workspace, status, provenance, and approval state should be easy to verify.
2. Keep the helm clear: the primary action and current task must remain obvious even when the system carries deep context.
3. Reveal complexity progressively: start with the lightest capable surface and expose advanced controls only when required.
4. Preserve continuity: conversations, jobs, artifacts, and decisions must remain recoverable and linked across sessions.
5. Earn trust through state: distinguish queued, running, blocked, ready, failed, approved, and archived work precisely.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion preferences, and status communication that never relies on color alone.
